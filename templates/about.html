About EdgeVision QC

EdgeVision QC is an AI-powered industrial quality inspection system designed to identify surface defects using computer vision and CNN-based image classification.

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • Database: SQLite
  • AI: TensorFlow, Keras, CNN
  • Computer Vision: OpenCV
  • Input/Classes: 224 × 224 images / 6 Classes
